Social Medicine Consortium 2023

Advancing health equity through social medicine practice and education

Since 2016, Social Medicine Consortiums have connected leaders, activists, educators, researchers, policymakers, and students within and outside the health sector to share best practices, build critical consciousness, and pursue global collective action for health equity.

From June 26-29, 2023, EqualHealth and Mbarara University hosted the 6th annual Social Medicine Consortium in Mbarara, Uganda, giving participants the opportunity to analyze the structural and social determinants of health and collaborate on innovative responses to global illness and inequity.

About the Consortium

What is the Social Medicine Consortium?

What is SMC?

A program of EqualHealth, the Social Medicine Consortium is a collective of committed individuals, universities, and organizations fighting for health equity through education, training, service, and advocacy, with social medicine at its core.

Who attends?

Leaders, activists, educators, civil society, researchers, policy makers, and students both within and outside the health sector come together to learn, share experiences, and collaborate on collective actions that center marginalized voices.

What will I learn?

Conferences focus on the principles and applications of social medicine, a framework that analyzes the social and structural determinants of health, cultivating collaboration across diverse systems and geographies.
