Our Programme

Building community consciousness on racism and health

As the Uganda Chapter of the EqualHealth Campaign Against Racism, we build community consciousness on the impact of racism and how it manifests and informs systems, structures, and everyday experiences in Uganda.

Our first community initiative, launched in 2018, grounded our work in reflection and action.

How We Engage

Reflection, dialogue, and action

01

Building Consciousness

We raise awareness of how racism shapes systems, structures, and everyday experiences that influence health and equity in Uganda.

02

Debate & Dialogue

We host student-led debates and dialogues that bring young voices together to question and unpack structural inequities.

03

Global South Solidarity

We connect local lived experience to a broader movement, in partnership with the EqualHealth Campaign Against Racism.
