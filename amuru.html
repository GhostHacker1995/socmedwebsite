Summary

Bringing care closer through mobility

The CareConnect Amuru project aims to enhance health equity in rural Uganda by addressing transportation barriers between Village Health Team members and local populations. We provide bicycles to enable community healthcare workers to reach community members more efficiently, facilitating timely healthcare delivery and referrals.

To ensure sustainability, we support a VHT-run bicycle maintenance workshop that generates income while serving as a hub for community health education and outreach.

An efficient health service delivery system in Amuru, Uganda.
Organising Statement

We are organising SocMed Global Uganda and the Amuru Village Health Team Association to achieve universal health care coverage in Uganda. We shall achieve this by improving the mobility of Village Health Team members and community health care workers to improve service delivery in communities by lobbying for bikes for VHTs.

We shall employ tactics of fundraising, lobbying, and a documentary on the journey of VHTs to document and showcase the challenges that VHTs encounter on a daily basis while executing their duties.

Strategic Objectives & Methods

How we deliver impact

1

Document the journey of VHTs

Capture the VHTs' daily service delivery on video, edited and published to boost the fundraising campaign.

2

Lobby & fundraise

Launch a campaign to raise 100,000 USD through the social medicine network, web donations, and grant applications.

3

Repair & purchase bikes

Repair repairable bikes and purchase new bikes to meet current demand, based on the availability of funds.

4

Monitor & evaluate

Measure impact through output and outcome indicators, using surveys and in-depth interviews.
