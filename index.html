SOCMED GLOBAL UGANDA

Health Equity for All

Challenged by the incessant disparities in health outcomes across population groups and geographies, we strive to reimagine equitable approaches to healthcare delivery, education and research that centers communities experiencing and living the effects of social, economic, cultural, geographical, and political disenfranchisement.

Inspired by the stories of self and that of their patients and communities, SocMed Global Uganda is a collective of like minded health professionals who live, practice, and accompany patients and communities through their illness journey across all corners of Uganda.

Our Vision

A Uganda free of health inequities

Our Mission

To promote health equity in Uganda, through practice, education, research, and empowerment

Our Slogan

SocMed Global Uganda "Health Equity for all"

OUR IMPACT

Challenging Structural Inequities in Health

Through global South coalition building, partnerships, and interdisciplinary organizing, we connect local lived experiences to global advocacy efforts aimed at advancing justice and dismantling systemic inequities.

READ MORE
Empowering a Conscious Generation of Young Leaders

Hosting student-led debates that bring together young voices to question, challenge, and unpack structural inequities, strengthening awareness and sparking meaningful conversations on justice and health equity.

READ MORE
Supporting Community Health Systems in Underserved Regions

We are helping frontline health workers reach households more quickly, improve referrals, and expand timely care to underserved communities in Amuru by equipping VHTs with bicycles.

READ MORE
Training the Next Generation of Equity-Focused Health Professionals

Annually, we support and deliver the Social Medicine Course as the Ugandan site alongside the other sites in Haiti and US, in partnership with Equal Health.

READ MORE
Care in the Midst of Crisis

When COVID-19 disrupted communities, in collaboration with EqualHealth, we supported frontline health workers in Amuru and Paimol communities with essential protective supplies.

READ MORE
Answering the Call in Karamoja

Together with our partners, we reached hundreds of households in Abim District, Karamoja region with life-sustaining food support, offering relief in a moment of urgent need.
