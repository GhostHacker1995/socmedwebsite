About the Role

Join our mission as Uganda Coordinator

SocMed Global Uganda is inviting applications for the position of Uganda Coordinator. As a collective of committed health professionals across Uganda, we strive to transform healthcare through community-centered education, delivery, and research.

What we're looking for

Degree in Public Administration, Medicine, Nursing, or Public Health

1-4 years of relevant experience
Excellent leadership, communication, and organization
Proficiency in Microsoft Office and Google Drive
